About Glengala Fresh

We Started With a
Simple Question.

Why does every "healthy" juice taste like compromise — watered down, processed, sitting on a shelf for weeks? We decided to answer it differently.

The Beginning

Born from a Fruit Shop.
Built Around Research.

Glengala Fresh started as the cold-pressed juice division of a fruit and vegetable shop. We were already handling the freshest local produce every day, so the question became obvious: what if we made something genuinely worth drinking?

We didn't start by guessing what flavours to combine. We started by asking what actually works. Eight ingredients examined against real health outcomes and real scientific literature — then formulated into combinations that make biological sense.

The result is six products, each built around a specific purpose. No filler formulas. No trend-chasing. Just combinations that make biological sense and taste like they should.

Our Process

Pressed Fresh, In Small Batches.

Whole fruit and vegetables, cold-pressed close to the day you drink it. Nothing added, nothing heat-treated.

A Note On Washing

We wash our produce in ozonated water before pressing. Ozone is used widely in food processing, and because it reverts to oxygen it does not leave a chlorine taste or sanitiser residue behind — which matters in a raw juice where there is nothing to hide the flavour.

That is a production step rather than something special, and we are not claiming a health benefit from it. If you want the detail, it is on our process page.

Our Sourcing

We Know Our
Growers.

We work directly with local produce suppliers we can trust. We ask them about how their produce was grown, prioritise low-spray options, and wash everything before pressing.

This direct relationship approach lets us maintain quality and transparency without the certification overhead.

The result: fresh, carefully sourced juice. No middleman markup. No bureaucracy. Just clean juice from people we know.

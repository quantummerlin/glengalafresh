Why Turmeric Is Paired With Pepper

Curcumin, the compound that gives turmeric its colour, is poorly absorbed by the body on its own. That is why turmeric is traditionally combined with a pungent companion ingredient — in this formula, cayenne. Lemon cuts the earthiness and honey makes it genuinely drinkable.

What's Inside

Four Ingredients.
That's The Lot.

No ginger in this formula. Golden Fire is turmeric-centred — cayenne for heat and the traditional pairing, lemon for brightness, and honey to make it easy to drink.

🌿

Turmeric

Fresh-pressed turmeric root. Deep golden colour and an earthy, slightly bitter flavour — the backbone of the whole formula.

🌶

Cayenne Pepper

Real heat. Traditionally paired with turmeric, and it gives this juice its warming finish.

🍋

Lemon

Vitamin C and acidity. Cuts through turmeric's earthiness and makes the whole thing bright rather than heavy.

🍯

Honey

Natural sweetness that rounds out the bitterness of raw turmeric and the heat of the cayenne.

Why People Drink It

What Makes Golden Fire Different

🧬

Real Turmeric, Pressed

Fresh turmeric root, not a powder suspended in water. That is where the deep golden colour and earthy flavour come from.

🔬

The Traditional Pairing

Turmeric is almost always served with a pungent companion ingredient across South Asian and wellness traditions. Cayenne plays that role here.

🦵

After Training

A warming drink people reach for after a session — the cayenne heat and the turmeric earthiness land well when you are tired.

🍛

With A Meal

Best taken alongside food rather than on an empty stomach. Many people drink it with lunch or dinner as a golden alternative to a soft drink.

🌶

Genuinely Spicy

This is not a mild golden latte. The cayenne is real and it is meant to be felt. If you want no heat, start with Immuni-Tea instead.

💧

Nothing Added

No concentrates, no refined sugar, no preservatives, no colourants. Nothing added after pressing.

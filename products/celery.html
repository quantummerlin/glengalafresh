What's Inside

Nothing Added.
Just Celery.

One ingredient: celery. No lemon to mask the flavour, no ginger to distract from it. Pressed whole from fresh celery, so you taste exactly what celery tastes like — salty, green and grassy.

🥬

Fresh Celery

Sourced from growers willing to tell us how their celery was grown. Celery is naturally high in water and potassium, which is where this juice's salty, savoury character comes from. Nothing else is added — we keep it as a single-ingredient drink by design.

Our produce is washed in ozonated water before pressing. It is a production step, not a health claim.

Why People Drink It

What Celery Week Is Actually For

🌿

One Ingredient, No Excuses

Nothing to blend, nothing to measure, nothing to hide behind. It is celery and that is the whole appeal for the people who commit to it.

💧

Morning Hydration

Celery is about 95% water with natural electrolytes. Drunk first thing on an empty stomach, it is a savoury way to start the day before coffee or food.

🗓️

Seven Days, One Habit

Celery Week is a routine, not a treatment. People use it to build one consistent morning habit over seven consecutive days and then decide whether it stays.

✨

Savoury, Not Sweet

If you are tired of sweet juice, this is the opposite — salty, green, grassy and genuinely unusual as a morning drink.

🧊

1L Bottles For The Week

Celery Week is ordered in 1L bottles so each day is one measured pour. Keep it refrigerated and drink within the use-by window on the bottle.

🥗

Nothing Concealed

No concentrates, no added sugars, no preservatives, no colourants, no filler juice. You are drinking pressed celery, and that is all you are drinking.

Straight Celery is a fresh unpasteurised juice. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and "Celery Week" is a routine rather than a health protocol. Celery is naturally high in sodium-like savoury compounds; if you are watching sodium intake, are pregnant, take medication, or are managing a health condition, check with your doctor or dietitian first.

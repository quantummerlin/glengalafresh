What's Inside

The Classic Trio.
Made Right.

Ginger, lemon and honey is one of the most loved drink combinations in the world. Immuni-Tea takes that combination, presses it fresh, and adds nothing else — our produce is washed in ozonated water before pressing, and that's the extent of the process.

🫚

Ginger

Fresh-pressed ginger root. Warm, spicy-sweet and the main flavour — without the sharp chilli burn of Immuni-Fire.

🍋

Lemon

Natural vitamin C from fresh fruit. Gives the drink its bright, clean acidity and cuts the ginger's sweetness.

🍯

Honey

Smooths out the ginger and lemon, adds natural sweetness without refined sugar, and makes it genuinely easy to drink every morning.

Our produce is washed in ozonated water before pressing. It is a production step, not a health claim.

What Makes It Easy To Drink Every Day

🫚

Warm Without Burn

All the ginger warmth, none of the chilli heat. This is the entry point for people who find spicy juices intense.

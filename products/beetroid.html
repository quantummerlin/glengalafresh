What's Inside

Four Ingredients.
Nothing Wasted.

Beetroid isn't just beetroot juice. It's a considered blend where each ingredient has a reason to be there — ginger for warmth and edge, lemon for brightness, honey to take the earthiness out of raw beetroot.

🥬

Beetroot

Fresh-pressed beetroot, naturally high in dietary nitrates. Earthy, sweet and deep purple — the backbone of the drink.

🫚

Ginger

Fresh ginger adds warmth and a spicy edge that cuts through beetroot's earthiness. It is also a classic gym-day flavour.

🍋

Lemon

Fresh lemon provides natural vitamin C and acidity. Lemon and beetroot is a classic pairing — vitamin C also helps the body absorb plant-based iron.

🍯

Honey

Natural sweetness that rounds out the earthy beetroot without refined sugar, and a quick carbohydrate source before training.

Our produce is washed in ozonated water before pressing. It is a production step, not a health claim.

Why People Drink It

What Makes Beetroid A Gym-Day Drink

🥬

Naturally Nitrate-Rich

Beetroot is one of the richest natural sources of dietary nitrates, which the body converts to nitric oxide. This is why beetroot juice is a staple around training, and it is the reason this product exists.

💪

Made For Before Training

Drink it 30–60 minutes before a session — that is the timing people who use beetroot juice around training actually use. The honey gives you quick carbohydrates too.

🍋

Balanced, Not Just Earthy

Raw beetroot alone is confronting for a lot of people. Lemon and ginger sharpen it, honey rounds it — the result tastes like a drink rather than a dare.

🔥

Ginger Warmth

Fresh ginger brings a spicy warmth that most beetroot juices lack — and ginger is one of the classic flavours people associate with gym routines.

🧠

Morning Alternative

People who do not train still drink it in the morning. It is earthy, sharp and genuinely different from a sweet orange juice — a savoury way to start the day.

💧

Nothing Hidden In It

No concentrates, no refined sugar, no preservatives, no added colour. Four ingredients, cold-pressed and bottled.

Beetroid is a fresh unpasteurised juice, not a medicine or a sports supplement. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Beetroot juice is naturally high in oxalates — if you have a history of kidney stones, or take medication such as blood pressure drugs, check with your doctor or dietitian first.

Best Results

When and How to Drink It

🏋️

Pre-Workout

Drink 30–60 minutes before training. That is when the nitrates in beetroot are doing their conversion work, and it is the timing most gym-goers use.

🌅

Morning Ritual

Not everyone drinks it around training. Plenty of people keep a bottle in the fridge as a savoury, earthy morning drink with breakfast.

🤸

Post-Match

After a game or session, the ginger warmth and honey sweetness land well as a recovery-flavoured drink, and the honey replaces some of what you burned.

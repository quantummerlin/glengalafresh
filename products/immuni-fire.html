What's Inside

Five Ingredients.
One Serious Shot.

Everything in Immuni-Tea — plus cayenne pepper. The cayenne changes the whole drinking experience: it turns a smooth, warming juice into something that genuinely wakes you up and leaves a heat on your palate that lingers.

🫚

Ginger

Fresh-pressed ginger root. Warm and spicy on its own, then amplified by the cayenne layered on top of it.

🍋

Lemon

Natural vitamin C from fresh fruit. Its acidity balances the heat and keeps the whole drink bright rather than heavy.

Honey

Smooths the burn and adds natural sweetness. This is what makes a genuinely hot drink easy to finish in one go.

Cayenne Pepper

The ingredient that makes this fire. Real, measurable heat — the defining difference from Immuni-Tea.

The Drinking Experience

What Makes This One Different

⚡

You Feel The Heat Straight Away

Cayenne lands on the palate almost immediately. This is not a subtle warming drink — it announces itself within the first sip.

🌬

Warming, Not Medicinal

People reach for this on cold mornings and in winter. The heat is the point — it is a warming drink, not a treatment.

🫚

Ginger Plus Cayenne

Two warming spices layered together instead of one. The ginger comes first, the cayenne follows and holds longer on the finish.

🍯

Honey Keeps It Drinkable

Raw honey and lemon acidity take the edge off the cayenne so a genuinely hot juice does not become an unpleasant one.

🛡

The Immuni-Tea Base

Same ginger, lemon and honey foundation as Immuni-Tea, with heat added on top. Try Immuni-Tea first if you do not like spicy drinks.

🔥

Controlled Heat Level

The cayenne is dosed to be noticeable but not punishing. Most people who choose this drink it daily without issue.
